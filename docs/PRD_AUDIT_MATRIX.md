## Order of work and evidence necessary for production release

**Release-critical dependency graph:** (1) owner independently proves provider rescue/VNC root login AND complete restorable encrypted offhost host+DB backups with a rollback trial; (2) vetted IdP/real MFA, narrow production PostgreSQL runtime, role membership and real tenant Site/Device/domain migration (including 0019 validated), independently current tenant/POP role tests; (3) production HTTPS on a legitimately controlled hostname or correctly trusted public-IP certificate strategy, narrow ingress, Host/SNI/cookie/CSRF negative tests, valid TLS and external browser access; (4) operator-managed domain proof/verification/ACME with a least-privilege reviewed reconciliation agent; (5) exact physical ACS/USP/OLT/ONT/MikroTik qualified operations with native recovery, audited approvals, full multi-node/backup/security acceptance. Do not repoint the existing lab SSH management hostname or expose `127.0.0.1:3002` publicly.

At every release, record **source SHA**, **environment (disposable vs actual public VPS vs physical device)**, command/test output, exact acceptance result, remaining blockers, changed files and safe rollback details. A screenshot of a form, a successful CI K3s fixture or `CONNECTED` without validated commands is not sufficient audit evidence of PRD completion.
